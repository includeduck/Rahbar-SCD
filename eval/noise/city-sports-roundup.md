# City Sports Roundup

*Fictional document for RAHBAR's noise test. Unrelated to universities.*

## Cricket

The city eleven won the final of the regional cup by six wickets after their opening pair put on
a century stand. The captain praised the bowlers for keeping the target below two hundred.

## Football

Rain delayed the derby by an hour, and the match ended goalless. Both coaches blamed the
waterlogged pitch.

## Marathon

More than three thousand runners took part in the spring marathon. The course was changed this
year to avoid the bridge, which is closed for repairs.
