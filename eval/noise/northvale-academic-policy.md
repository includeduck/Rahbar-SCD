# Northvale Institute of Technology: Academic Policy

*Fictional document for RAHBAR's noise test. Not about FAST-NUCES.*

## Attendance

Students at Northvale must attend at least 75% of the classes in each course. The Dean may
excuse up to 10% further absence for medical reasons. A student below the limit is barred from
the final examination of that course.

## Academic standing

A student whose cumulative grade point average falls below 1.75 is placed on academic probation.
A student on probation for two consecutive terms is dismissed from the programme.

## Repeating courses

A failed course may be repeated at most twice, in any later term. Both grades remain on the
transcript and the higher one is used in the grade point average.

## Course load

The normal load is six courses per term. Students with a grade point average above 3.5 may take
a seventh course with the approval of their adviser.

## Final year project

Credits for a final year project completed at another institution may be transferred if the
project committee approves the report.
