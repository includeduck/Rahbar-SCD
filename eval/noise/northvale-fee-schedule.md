# Northvale Institute of Technology: Fee Schedule

*Fictional document for RAHBAR's noise test. Not about FAST-NUCES.*

## Tuition

Undergraduate tuition is Rs. 9,000 per credit hour. Graduate tuition is Rs. 11,000 per credit
hour. Tuition is charged in two instalments each term.

## Admission and deposits

The admission fee is Rs. 20,000. A refundable library deposit of Rs. 5,000 is charged once.

## Refunds

A course dropped in the first two weeks of a term is refunded in full. After the second week
half of the tuition is refunded, and nothing after the fourth week.

## Hostel

Hostel rooms cost Rs. 45,000 per term, including meals. Applications open one month before the
term begins.
