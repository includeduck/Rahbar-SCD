# Course Withdrawal FAQ

*Synthetic test document for RAHBAR (Assignment 03, Batch B). Not an official FAST-NUCES document.*

## How do I withdraw from a course?

Submit the course withdrawal form on the student portal before the withdrawal deadline, which is
the end of the 12th week of a regular semester. The course instructor and the academic advisor
must both approve the request.

## What grade appears on my transcript?

A withdrawn course is shown with the grade W. A W does not count towards the GPA or the CGPA,
but the course still counts as attempted.

## Do I get a refund?

No tuition fee is refunded for a course withdrawn after the add and drop period.

## Can I withdraw from every course?

No. A student must stay registered in at least one course in a regular semester, otherwise the
request is treated as a semester freeze.
