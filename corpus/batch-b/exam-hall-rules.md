# Examination Hall Rules

*Synthetic test document for RAHBAR (Assignment 03, Batch B). Not an official FAST-NUCES document.*

## 1. Entry and seating

1.1 Students must reach the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the paper starts.
1.2 A student who arrives more than 30 minutes after the start is not allowed to enter.
1.3 Seats are allotted by roll number; the seating plan is displayed outside the hall.

## 2. Items allowed

2.1 Only transparent pencil boxes, a non-programmable calculator and the student ID card are
allowed at the desk.
2.2 Mobile phones and smart watches must be switched off and left in the bag at the front of the
hall. A phone found on a student's person is treated as an attempt to use unfair means.

## 3. Leaving the hall

3.1 No student may leave during the first hour of a paper.
3.2 A student who finishes early hands the answer book to the invigilator and leaves quietly.
