# Library Services Guide

*Synthetic test document for RAHBAR (Assignment 03, Batch B). Not an official FAST-NUCES document.*

## Opening hours

The main library is open from 8:00 to 20:00 on weekdays and from 9:00 to 17:00 on Saturdays.
It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Borrowing

Undergraduate students may borrow up to three books at a time for 14 days. A late return is
fined Rs. 50 per book per day.
